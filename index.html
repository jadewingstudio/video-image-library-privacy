摘要：Video Image Library 是本機桌面應用程式。Jadewing Studio 不會透過本應用程式收集、上傳、出售或分享您的圖片、影片、收藏庫資料或使用狀況。

1. 適用範圍

本隱私權政策適用於由 Jadewing Studio 發行的 Video Image Library（以下稱「本應用程式」），說明本應用程式如何存取及處理資料。

2. 本應用程式存取的資料

本應用程式只在您主動選取或設定的情況下，存取電腦上的圖片、影片、資料夾及磁碟位置，以建立及顯示媒體收藏庫。為提供收藏、搜尋、封面、縮圖、PIN 與備份等功能，本應用程式可能在您的裝置上處理或保存：

  • 您選取的媒體檔案路徑、檔名及基本檔案資訊；
  • 收藏庫、收藏項目與媒體集的索引及設定；
  • 由媒體檔案產生的封面及縮圖快取；
  • 介面語言、外觀與其他應用程式偏好設定；
  • PIN 的不可逆雜湊驗證資料及復原碼的雜湊驗證資料；
  • 發生未預期錯誤時建立於本機的診斷記錄。

3. 資料儲存與傳輸

上述資料均儲存在您的本機裝置，主要位於 Windows 的本機應用程式資料目錄。除非您自行使用其他軟體或服務移動、同步或分享檔案，否則本應用程式不會將媒體、索引、快取、PIN 驗證資料、診斷記錄或使用資料傳送給 Jadewing Studio 或第三方。本應用程式不包含開發者營運的分析、廣告或遙測服務。

4. 備份

若您使用備份功能，備份檔會儲存到您選擇的位置。備份可包含收藏庫索引、應用程式設定，以及在您選擇完整備份時包含封面與縮圖快取；原始圖片與影片不包含在備份中。您可選擇使用密碼加密備份。備份檔及其復原金鑰的保管、複製、同步與分享由您自行控制。

5. PIN 與安全性

PIN 用於限制本應用程式介面的存取。PIN 本身不會以明文保存，但 PIN 不會加密或限制其他程式對原始媒體檔案及未加密快取的存取。請使用 Windows 帳號權限、裝置加密或其他適當措施保護您的檔案。

6. 診斷記錄

本應用程式在發生未預期錯誤時，可能在本機建立包含應用程式版本、錯誤類型與技術堆疊資訊的診斷記錄。記錄會嘗試遮蔽使用者個人目錄及磁碟路徑。診斷記錄不會自動上傳；只有在您自行選擇提供給支援人員時才會離開您的裝置。

7. 資料保留與刪除

資料會保留在您的裝置上，直到您在本應用程式內刪除相關收藏庫、清除快取，或透過 Windows 解除安裝並移除相關本機應用程式資料。刪除收藏庫索引不會刪除原始圖片或影片。您另行儲存的備份檔必須由您從其保存位置自行刪除。

8. 第三方平台

透過 Microsoft Store 下載、安裝或更新本應用程式時，Microsoft 可能依其自身的隱私權聲明處理帳戶、裝置、商店及診斷資料。該處理行為由 Microsoft 負責，並非由本應用程式控制。

9. 兒童隱私

本應用程式不要求建立帳號，也不會刻意向兒童收集個人資料。

10. 政策變更

若本應用程式的資料處理方式或功能有所變更，本政策可能隨之更新。更新版本會公布於本頁，並標示新的生效日期。

11. 聯絡方式

如對本隱私權政策有任何問題，請聯絡 Jadewing Studio：jadewing.studio@outlook.com

English

Summary: Video Image Library is a local desktop application. Jadewing Studio does not use the app to collect, upload, sell, or share your images, videos, library data, or usage activity.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to Video Image Library (the “App”), published by Jadewing Studio, and explains how the App accesses and processes data.

2. Data Accessed by the App

The App accesses images, videos, folders, and drive locations on your computer only when you select or configure them, so it can create and display your media libraries. To provide library, collection, search, cover, thumbnail, PIN, and backup features, the App may process or store the following on your device:

  • Paths, file names, and basic file information for media you select;
  • Library, collection, and Media Set indexes and settings;
  • Cover and thumbnail caches generated from media files;
  • Language, appearance, and other App preferences;
  • Non-reversible verification hashes for the PIN and recovery code; and
  • Local diagnostic logs created when an unexpected error occurs.

3. Storage and Transmission

This data is stored locally on your device, primarily in the Windows local application data directory. Unless you independently use another program or service to move, synchronize, or share files, the App does not transmit media, indexes, caches, PIN verification data, diagnostic logs, or usage data to Jadewing Studio or any third party. The App does not include developer-operated analytics, advertising, or telemetry services.

4. Backups

When you use the backup feature, the backup file is saved to a location you choose. Backups may contain library indexes and App settings, and a complete backup may include cover and thumbnail caches if you select that option. Original images and videos are not included. You may choose to password-encrypt a backup. You control the storage, copying, synchronization, and sharing of backup files and their recovery keys.

5. PIN and Security

The PIN restricts access to the App interface. The PIN itself is not stored in plain text, but it does not encrypt or restrict access by other programs to original media files or unencrypted caches. Use Windows account permissions, device encryption, or other appropriate measures to protect your files.

6. Diagnostic Logs

When an unexpected error occurs, the App may create a local diagnostic log containing the App version, error type, and technical stack information. The App attempts to redact the user profile directory and drive paths. Diagnostic logs are not uploaded automatically and leave your device only if you choose to provide them to support.

7. Retention and Deletion

Data remains on your device until you delete the relevant library in the App, clear its cache, or uninstall the App and remove its associated local application data through Windows. Deleting a library index does not delete original images or videos. Backup files saved elsewhere must be deleted by you from their chosen locations.

8. Third-Party Platforms

When you download, install, or update the App through Microsoft Store, Microsoft may process account, device, Store, and diagnostic data under its own privacy statement. Microsoft is responsible for that processing, which is not controlled by the App.

9. Children's Privacy

The App does not require an account and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy if the App's features or data practices change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

11. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, contact Jadewing Studio at jadewing.studio@outlook.com.
